# Hakkımızda görselleri v1

Üç görsel yerleşik image_gen aracıyla ayrı ayrı üretildi. Gerçek işletme, mekân veya personel fotoğrafı değildir; yapay zekâyla oluşturulmuş temsili sahnelerdir. Gerçek işletmenin mekânları/personeli olarak sunulmamalıdır.

- 01-cilt-bakimi-uzman.png: Büyük dikey fotoğraf alanı. Kemer maskesini kodda uygulayın; iki yüzü ve fırça temasını koruyun.
- 02-resepsiyon-temsili.png: Sağ üst yatay alan.
- 03-bakim-odasi-temsili.png: Sağ alt yatay alan.

Fotoğraflarda yazı, logo, tabela veya filigran yoktur. Fotoğraflara sonradan Esteelitta yazısı eklemeyin. Çerçeveler, rozet, ikonlar ve bölüm metinleri HTML/CSS/SVG ile ayrı oluşturulmalıdır. Bu klasör dosya teslimidir; ana site kodu değiştirilmedi.

## Üretim promptları

### 1

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create ONE finished photographic asset for the large portrait photograph in the About section of an elegant beauty salon website. Portrait 2:3 composition, full-bleed rectangular photograph, high resolution. Fictional representative scene, not an actual business or its staff. A female adult aesthetician in a clean unbranded white short-sleeved tunic, dark brown hair neatly tied in a bun, gently applying a white facial mask to the adult female client's cheek with a small flat cosmetic brush. Aesthetician standing behind and slightly to the right of reclining client, both faces and anatomically correct gloved hands clearly visible. Client relaxed with closed eyes, hair held back by a plain white headband, body modestly covered by a thick white towel on a white treatment chair. Professional, calm, natural expressions, realistic skin pores and fabric texture, believable hands and brush contact on cheek, no needles or lasers. Bright modern treatment room with white cabinetry and subtle pale aqua frosted glass, diffuse window daylight from left, neutral whites, soft aqua accents, natural warm skin tones, restrained premium editorial photography, no plastic skin or glamour retouching. Frame from client's covered upper torso to above aesthetician's head; leave ample headroom and keep both faces and brush contact within the central 70 percent so a tall arch-shaped website mask will not cut them off. Background softly out of focus but credible. Absolutely NO text, lettering, signs, numbers, logos, branding, watermark, wall graphics, or Esteelitta word anywhere, including clothing, equipment, bottles and walls. No border, arch mask, icons, collage, layout, or website UI. Output only this one photograph.
